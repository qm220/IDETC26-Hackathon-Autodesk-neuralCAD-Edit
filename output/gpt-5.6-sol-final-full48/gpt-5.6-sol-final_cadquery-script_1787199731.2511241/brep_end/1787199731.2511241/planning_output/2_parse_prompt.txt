You are given the multiview images of a engineering CAD model.

An engineer has requested an edit to the model with the following natural language request:
```
Scale the part 10x. Add drafts of 2 degrees to all vertical surfaces, with flat bottom surface as a hinge. Add rounds R=1mm to all inner edges and R=3mm to all outer edges. Exclude edges, inner or outer, on the flat bottom surface. Add two cylinder features with D=6mm and inner hole D=3mm. Cylinders should be spaced 30mm apart and shall be placed centered with respect to the main part. They should start on the flat bottom surface level and reach all the way to the part wall on the top side
```

You will analyse this request and produce a aim.json file to describe the aim of this request. 

1. Categorize each word in the request into four categories:
(1). Action words, describing a geometric operation, such as createing a new feature, includes numerical parameters of that operation.
(2). Referenced geometries, referring to existing geometry, features, entities, or relationships that must be resolved from the original CAD model.
(3). Intention, describing the engineering purpose of the modification and the desired function, or desired behavior of the final object produced.
(4). grammatical words, articles, conjunctions, prepositions, and other words without independent engineering meaning. Do not discard relational words that may have engineering meaning such as "connecting", "between", "through", "across", "adjacent", or "aligned". Preserve them as geometric/relational constraints when they affect how the edit should be performed.

2. Identify areas to change
Based on the multiview images, describe the areas that need to be changed and predict the appearance, function and behavior of the outcome in concise natural language.

3. Identify areas to preserve
Based on the multiview images, describe the areas that need to be preserved in concise natural language.

4. Record your analysis in the JSON output following the JSON schema

OUTPUT (Return ONLY valid JSON.)
